\begin{center}
{\noindent{\bf Abstract}}
\end{center}


 People have begun to convert their mind in choosing a way of moving as living standard rising, and the people to go abroad have increased rapidly as the trend of economic globalization,have brought great pressure to airlines to increase their capacity to meet the transport demand, so the slimline seats on the airplane emerge at the historic moment. It is because that the people have begun to consider making the comfort on the body and mind as the primary as nowadays, $'$humanization$'$ should be put on a great attention so that he airline can triumph in the $'$space war$'$ whichever can does well in designing the most comfortable seat back even though it has a thinner backplate and less padding.So how to design a seat back curve to increase the comfort of slimline seats is a problem need to be solved urgently.
%由于人与人之间的脊椎情况并不完全相同，这与年龄、性别、种族等等因素有关，故本文将问题考虑重点放在座椅曲线的设计方法上，并未采集大量数据进行处理。本文通过C++程序编写，从普通正常人体的脊椎图像上提取点并将其坐标化和数字化，以此作为数据点。通过分析找到一种较优的曲线拟合方式使得所拟合出来的曲线更加否合人体脊椎的生理曲线。本文通过分析研究发现用最小二乘的思想所拟合出来的高斯曲线更加与人体的脊椎生理曲线相吻合，故给出了高斯曲线拟合模型。随后，本文对休息时人体坐姿进行了抽象化，结合生物工程学对其进行理论分析，得到了个受力点并对其进行了受力分析。再根据查阅资料所得信息进行综合给出了一个非线性最优化模型，利用进行编程运算，得到了受力点的位置信息，利用高斯曲线拟合模型对其进行曲线拟合，并对其进行平滑优化，保证这5个点分别对应为曲线仅有的极大（小）值点，以此曲线作为人在自然弯曲状态下的脊椎曲线，故与此相贴合的座椅靠背曲线便是让人感觉最舒适的。

The interpersonal spines are different in the modalities, it is related to so many factors, in this thesis we will focus on the design method of seat back curve, not collect a large amount of data to process. Through the analysis of studies, we found that the curve fitted by the least squares Gauss curve fitting method is in conformity with the physiological curve of spine, so we bring out the Gauss curve fitting model. And then, introduces the abstraction of body posture in resting, carries on the theoretical analysis combined with biological engineering and receives five stress points and analyses them. According to the information, we gives a nonlinear optimization model and uses Lingo 14.0 to calculate, obtains the location information of the stress points, using Gauss curve fitting model for curve fitting, and carries on the smooth optimization to ensure the five points are the sole extremum points of the curve corresponding, this curve is been considered as a man$^{'}$s spinal curve in the condition of nature, at the same time, the joint curve is the best seat back curve to let a person feel more comfortable.

%由于非线性最优化模型中的受力点的选取方式比较粗略，故本文考虑对其进行进一步优化，提出了基于体压分布来进行考虑的优化模型，在这一模型中，对最大压力、平均压力、最大压力梯度、平均压力梯度、不对称系数、纵向压力分布曲线、纵向力矩分布曲线及舒适性系数个指标进行刻画，通过压力传感技术对不同坐姿（在一定范围内）所引起的压强变化进行动态监测得到大量的实验数据，然后对这些数据进行统计及处理分析，找到舒适度达到较优时脊椎各部分所对应的压强波动范围，在时间序列法的理念基础上对其进行修改，得到脊椎各部分所受压力随靠背角度变化而产生的变化情况的模型，进而对其进行分析预测，结合舒适度评价模型，找到靠背在可调整角度范围内变化时舒适度达到最高的靠背倾角以及此时人体的体压分布情况，将其数字化后便可得到此时人体脊椎受压分布情况，反推得到贴合这一脊椎的座椅靠背曲线。
Due to the method of the stress point in the nonlinear optimization model system is sketchy, this thesis considers carrying on a further optimization, so we bring out a new optimization model based on body pressure distribution. In this model, we define 8 indexes to discuss. If the condition permitted we can obtain a lot of data caused by pressure change on different posture(within a certain range) dynamically by the use of the pressure sensor technology, make statistics and analysis, and then find a pressure range in which comfort level can achieve a higher point. After that, making some modification on the basis of the concept of time series method, get pressure data with the change of the angle between the seat back and seat  of each part of spine resulting in the model, and then analyzes and predicts in combination with comfort evaluation model, find out the angle of seat backplate that comfort level can achieve a high place within the scope of the adjustable angle as well as the human body pressure distribution, at this time, the pressure distribution on human$^{'}$s spine can be got after its digital, push reversely to get the seat back curve jointed better with the modality of spine.

%由于时间和技术限制的原因，本文所提出的综合模型没有时间和数据进行具体操作实验，只提出了基本思想，但由文献  可以保证此种想法具有可实施性，在条件允许的前提下可以得到较优的结果，并且可以通过结合有限元的思想用Matlab实现人工智能模拟。
Because of time and technical limitations, the comprehensive model this thesis proposed only be put forward the basic thoughts, but this model is practical which can be inferred from the literature. Simulation of artificial intelligence perhaps can be realized if this model can be blended in the idea of finite element method. Thus, it is the task we are likely to attempt.

\section{The Description of Problem}

An airline seat is a seat on an airliner in which passengers are accommodated for the duration of the journey. Some airlines are now introducing new $'$slimline$'$ seats in economy class. These seats,in addition to weighing less, theoretically allow airlines to increase capacity without significantly affecting passenger comfort. These seats may or may not feature moveable headrests, and generally do not feature adjustable lumbar support. Slimline seats are being further refined, liberating more passenger space. The common point of them is a thinner backplate and less padding. However, many passengers have expressed displeasure with these seats.

{\bf Task 1:} Without changing the structure of the premise, how to design seat back curve, in order to make the seat more comfortable?

%针对这一问题，因为时间有限我们并不能获取大量的不同人体的脊椎有关数据，故本文考虑将重点放在方法的提出上，所需数据我们选择通过编写程序将人体脊椎图像数字化并坐标化。
In order to solve this problem, we can not get a lot of data of different human spine because of the limited time. Therefore, this paper consider focusing on method of put forward.What${'}$s more, we find the required data by writing C program which can make the human body vertebra image digitization and coordinated.


$\bullet$%随机抽取得到一个点列，对其进行多种曲线拟合，得出自然状态下人的脊椎曲线，将这些曲线与人的脊椎图像放在同一图像下，判断哪种拟合方式更加合适。
We randomly pick some points from human spine picture to make a scatter diagram. Next we choose some different curve to fit the scatter diagram. So we get some approximate human spine curve in natural condition. Then we compare these curves with human spine image to pick a better curve preliminarily.

$\bullet$%对人体脊椎构造进行抽象分析，找到受力点分别对其进行受力分析，根据对已有文献的分析找到问题所需参数的取值及变量的取值范围，建立非线性优化模型找出受力点，再将其进行坐标化得到受力点的位置坐标。利用上一步骤中所选取的较优的函数拟合类型对其进行拟合，得到我们想要的座椅靠背曲线。
By abstracting analysis on the human body vertebra structures, we have found points respectively on the force analysis, based on the analysis of the existed literature to find the values of parameters which are required and the scope of variables, and nonlinear optimization model is established to find points, then find the coordinates of the stress point according to make them coordinate. Now we can take advantage of the  former step in the selection of the optimal function fitting types on the fitting to get the seat backrest curve which we expect.

{\bf Task 2:} How to optimize the seat backplate curve and padding, in order to make the seat more comfortable, without changing the main internal structure?

%针对这个问题我们将在上述非线性优化模型下对其进行进一步的优化，由于我们在上述模型中对其进行的是受力点的粗略估计及受力分析，在这一过程中必然会出现较大的误差，故在此阶段我们考虑运用压力传感技术得到不同坐姿的体压分布情况，通过软件分析得到最舒适座椅靠背曲线并用神经网络方法给出评价。
 To solve this problem preferably, we further optimize the model above. In this present model, we just calculate the approximate index of force bearing points and do the rough force analysis, so the error will be dissatisfactory. To minimize the error, we use pressure sensing technology to get seat$^{’}$s pressure distributions of different human sitting posture. And then, we code program to analyze these pressure data. Next, using these data to optimize our curve model. At the end, we build a neural network to evaluate our curve model.

\end{eqnarray}

In the above type $\omega(x)\geq0$ is the weight function on $[a,b]$ 上的权函数,它表示不同点it stands the differences in the proportion of different data $(x_{i},f(x_{i}))$ ,并且这里and here ,
